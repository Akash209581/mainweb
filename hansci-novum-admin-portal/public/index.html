Accommodation & partnerships

Can I stay at the conference venue?

Choose Accommodation for a room-only request, or add venue accommodation to any registration, exhibition or sponsorship enquiry. Select dates, room type and number of rooms. Availability, venue details and booking terms must be confirmed before a reservation is made. Accommodation-only requests do not include conference admission.

How are exhibition and sponsorship packages priced?

Select your preferred exhibition format or sponsorship tier and provide your organisation’s details. Packages require an individual quote. Prices and benefits will be confirmed separately.

Are the currency prices live conversions?

No. USD, EUR and GBP show illustrative prices configured for this preview. Final prices in each currency must be approved before payments are enabled.

Before you register

Does registration include abstract acceptance?

No. Scientific submissions are reviewed separately. An abstract reference can be added to your registration, but payment does not guarantee acceptance.

What is included in the registration fee?

The inclusions above demonstrate the proposed layout. Confirmed inclusions will be published for the specific conference. Travel and accommodation should be booked separately unless explicitly included.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

The conference-specific cancellation deadlines and refund terms have not yet been configured. They must be published and acknowledged before payment is enabled.

Can I pay on behalf of my institution?

The form supports institutional invoice details and a purchase-order reference. Available payment methods will be confirmed when registration opens.
