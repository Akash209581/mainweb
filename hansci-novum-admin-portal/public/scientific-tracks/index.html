Scientific programme questions

Are these the final sessions?

No. These are proposed hematology themes. Final session titles, speakers, chairs and schedules require confirmation by the scientific committee.

Can I submit interdisciplinary research?

Yes. Choose the closest theme when submitting. The committee can confirm the most appropriate track.

Does selecting a track guarantee acceptance?

No. Abstracts are reviewed separately. Track selection and presentation preference do not confirm acceptance or a particular presentation format.
