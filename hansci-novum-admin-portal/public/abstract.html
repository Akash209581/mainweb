Submission questions

Can I change my presentation preference?

In this preview you can return to the form and change your preference. The scientific committee will determine the final presentation format when the review process is live.

Does abstract acceptance include registration?

No. Registration is a separate process. Any applicable presenter registration requirements will be published for the conference.

Are my details saved?

Submitted abstracts and documents are saved securely. Unsubmitted form entries are cleared when the page is refreshed or closed.
